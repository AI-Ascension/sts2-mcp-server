# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to act professionally, assume good faith while resolving ambiguity, and keep
technical discussion focused on evidence, safety, accessibility, and the target's documented boundary.
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberate exposure of
credentials or private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the project's approved security or
maintainer channel. Include only the context needed to investigate; do not attach credentials, saves,
proprietary game files, or personal data. Maintainers may clarify, remove harmful material, pause
participation, or take other proportionate action.

This code of conduct applies in repository spaces and in project-related communication. Contributions
remain subject to [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the applicable license.
